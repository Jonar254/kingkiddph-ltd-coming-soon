What we do

One partner. Every finish.

Most suppliers do one thing. A tile shop. A hardware importer. A contractor. Form & Finish sits at the point where design selection, procurement, technical specification, and execution meet — so your project gets the right product, properly coordinated, all the way to completion.

01
Selection & Specification
We help you choose the right materials for each space — finishes, formats, budgets, and style. No guesswork. No wrong purchases.
02
Managed Sourcing
We source internationally — China, Turkey, Italy, India — vetting suppliers, coordinating samples, and managing import logistics on your behalf.
03
Supply & Accessories
Everything around the main product — trims, adhesives, hardware, handles, profiles, underlays. The complete system, not just the headline item.
04
Fit-Out Coordination
We coordinate trades, review measurements, oversee installation, and review the final finish. Your project delivered, not just delivered to site.
